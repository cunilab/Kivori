The first Kivori beta for Windows.

- Turn the knob for volume, press it to play or pause, hold it to mute.
- Three buttons for previous, play or pause, and next, ready to use.
- Profiles that follow the app in front of you, such as a browser, a code editor or a video call.
- Five display views: Buddy, Clock, Volume, Media and System.
- A buddy with moods that reacts to what your computer is doing.
- Change what any control does, or chain steps into a macro and run it with one press.
- Honest status lights after every action: Confirmed, Started, Unverified or Error.
- Starts with Windows and sits quietly in the tray.
- Goes to sleep when your computer sleeps or locks, and wakes up with it.
- Update your Kivori from the app, and restore it from the app if an update ever goes wrong.
- A short first-run setup that walks you through plugging in and trying the controls.
